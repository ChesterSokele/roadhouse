    Our Story

    Born from the love of fire & flavour

    Roadhouse Deli & Grill is more than a restaurant — it's a gathering place in the heart of Windhoek. By day we fuel the city with hearty meals and honest food. By night we light up the bar with craft cocktails, premium spirits, and the kind of atmosphere that keeps you coming back. Warm wood, bold flavours, and a staff that wear black because they mean business.
